WHERE TO START

Choose your next step

  1. Identify the support you need

    Housing, treatment with children, parenting classes, and supervised visits are different services. For emergency shelter, call 211. Mothers, fathers, and kinship caregivers can start with a family resource center.

  2. Check the referral route

    Ask whether you can apply yourself or need a referral. If you have a DCYF case or court order, check with your assigned worker and lawyer about services that fit your plan before enrolling.

  3. Confirm the practical details

    Ask who can attend, children’s age limits, whether children can stay overnight, fees, waitlists, transportation, and the visit or intake location. A map pin does not mean a bed or appointment is available.

What should I say when I call?A short script for parents and caregivers.
Hi, I’m looking for [family housing / parenting support / supervised visits]. I’m a [parent / kinship caregiver] with children ages [ages]. Who is eligible, do I need a referral, and what are the costs and next steps? If I’m working toward reunification, can your program support my current case plan?

Share case documents only through the provider’s approved process. Do not enter client, child, custody, or health information into this resource hub.

These listings are starting points, not legal advice or a guarantee of reunification. General housing and parenting programs are not automatically approved visitation or reunification services. Ask your lawyer about legal questions.
