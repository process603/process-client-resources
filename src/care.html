A SIMPLE PLAN

Three steps to get connected

    What should I say when I call?A short script you can read or adapt.
    What should I have ready?A simple checklist for your first appointment.
    • Your insurance card, if you have one, and a photo ID if available. Ask the office what to do if something is missing.
    • A safe callback number and the town where you will be living.
    • A medication list, pharmacy name, and any discharge or referral paperwork you have.
    • Your questions, transportation needs, and interpreter or accessibility requests.
    • Ask how to transfer records and whether you need a referral. Do not send private health details through this resource hub.
    Moving after treatment or using telehealth?Choose care you can keep using.

    Search near the town where you will live after treatment. Check travel time and transportation before booking. For telehealth, tell the office which state you will physically be in during appointments and ask whether they can see you there. Confirm insurance coverage, device requirements, and a private place to talk.
